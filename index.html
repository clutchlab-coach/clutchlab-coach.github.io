The 5-Minute Pre-Game Reset

Five small steps, done together before the game. That is the whole reset.

  1. 1. Breathe Breathe in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4, for 60 seconds. “Slow breath. Clear mind.”
  2. 2. Visualize Visualize one key play done well.
  3. 3. Pick a cue word Pick a cue word — smooth, strong, or locked in — and say it 3 times.
  4. 4. Reset your body Shake out, shoulders back twice, loosen your jaw, stand tall. “I’m ready.”
  5. 5. Choose one focus Choose one focus target for the first five minutes.
Made a mistake in the game?
One slow breath. Say your cue word. Find the next moment.
